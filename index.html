How the answer works

The site compares the Pokémon's six base stats (HP, Attack, Defense, Sp. Attack, Sp. Defense and Speed) with the stats of what it evolves into.

  • Yes, evolve it! The total goes up and no stat drops by 20 or more.
  • Yes, but think about it. The total goes up or stays the same, but at least one stat drops by 20 or more.
  • No, press B! The total goes down. In the games, pressing B stops an evolution.

The data has no move lists or evolution levels, so the answer is based on stats only.
